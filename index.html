FE-PTL-001 Active Hard PyTorch / Pandas

Point-in-Time Feature Leakage

Repair a forecasting feature pipeline whose validation metrics are inflated by lookahead bias.

Production Problem & Symptoms

Known Symptoms

Validation loss collapses near zero in backtests, but live paper trading experiences severe drawdown. Adding random noise to t+1 alters feature vector at time t.

Expected Behavior

All rolling window aggregations must be strictly computed using data available at or before cutoff timestamp t - 1. Predictions must be invariant to perturbation of future time steps.
